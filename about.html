About

Made with Love, Crafted by Hand

Hi, I’m Nandani Mehtre. I make soft, thoughtful crochet pieces in small batches, one stitch at a time, for people who like gifts with a story.

🧶
Our story

It started with a rainy season

I picked up my first crochet hook on a long, rainy week indoors. My first attempt was a lopsided flower, and I loved it anyway.

Friends started asking for flowers, then little bunnies, then gifts with their names on them. What began as a quiet hobby slowly turned into Z_Stitch..

Everything here is inspired by small, happy things: garden flowers, childhood toys, and the feeling of getting something made just for you.

Our craft

Slow, soft and made by hand

Every piece begins with choosing the yarn: soft cotton blends that are gentle on skin. I sketch the shape, stitch it row by row, then stuff, sew and finish each one by hand. A single plushie takes a few evenings.
